Important interpretation notes
- realSum is the full accommodation price for two people and two nights in EUR, including reservation and cleaning fees according to the associated paper. It is not a nightly price.
- Each city has separate weekday and weekend offer samples. The source files have no observation date column and no reliable Airbnb listing identifier.
- The unnamed first column is a zero-based row index exported with each CSV. It restarts in every file and must not be treated as an Airbnb listing ID.
- Filename city and weekday/weekend labels are source metadata and should later be engineered into explicit lineage columns without changing the raw files.
